/01 · Engagements

Four kinds of work. Most engagements mix two.

I judge the work by what is still running six months later.

/01.A

Strategic clarity

Most teams have too many priorities. I help you pick the two or three that matter and drop the rest. You come out with a decision, written down, with someone who owns it.

/01.B

AI adoption

Working out where AI is actually worth using in your team, and putting some simple rules around it. I've done this inside a large company, so it stays practical.

/01.C

Programme rescue

When a programme slips and people stop trusting the plan, I fix governance first and then get the team shipping again. I did this with a Data Warehouse that went live after going badly off-track.

/01.D

Operating model & cost

Who decides what, how teams work together and what it all costs. I advise a CFO and COO on exactly this every week, and I've cut annual costs by 55% without the operation falling over.

/03 · About

Mechanical engineer by training. Strategy and operations ever since.

I started at Accenture, rolling out Workday from Lisbon and Singapore. Then four years at EY, where I helped start the Electricity Sector practice and worked with utilities in Portugal, Angola and Mozambique. Since 2021 I've been at Mercedes-Benz.io. I'm Head of Corporate Strategy & Growth, I sit on the Executive Board, and I lead a team of seven across Portugal and Germany, working directly with the CEO.

The work tends to follow the same pattern. There's a problem nobody has framed properly, a few busy people who need to decide, and then the slow part of making the decision stick. Most strategy falls apart in that last stretch. It's also the part I enjoy most.

Outside work I build things. I maintain jobkit, an open-source job-search plugin for Claude Code, and I've had a homelab running for eight years. These days it's mostly where I try out new tools.

I live in Porto and work with founders anywhere. I only take on a few engagements at a time.
